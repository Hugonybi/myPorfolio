    How it works

    • Type a task and press Enter or Add task. Empty tasks are rejected.
    • Tick the checkbox, or the task text, to mark it as done.
    • Use the filters to show all, to-do or completed tasks.
    • Delete removes one task. Clear completed removes every finished task.
    • Tasks past their due date are shown in red.

    Tasks are stored in this browser only.
